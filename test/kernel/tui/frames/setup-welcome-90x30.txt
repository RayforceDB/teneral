 teneral  setup

  Welcome

  This sets up where the agent's model comes from: you choose a provider, give
  it a key or sign in, and two test requests are made. Nothing is written
  until they have worked.

  Where does the model come from?

  ▸ 1  ChatGPT plan (Continue with ChatGPT)
       Uses your ChatGPT Plus or Pro plan for the agent's requests, through
       OpenAI's sign-in for open-source apps that run locally.
       You sign in on a page at OpenAI in your own browser. teneral never sees
       your password.
       The tokens OpenAI issues are stored in .teneral/auth/, readable only by
       you, and by the agent's own shell.
       What the agent uses counts against your plan; review and limit it at
       https://chatgpt.com/settings/usage

    2  OpenAI API key
    3  Another OpenAI-compatible endpoint
    4  Kimi Code plan (API key from the Kimi Code Console)
